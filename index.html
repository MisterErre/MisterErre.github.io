Background

I started out fixing machines and networks in a museum basement, which is a more literal beginning than most IAM careers get. Consulting came next — SIA, then KPMG — where I learned the trade on SailPoint by delivering for banks and energy companies that do not enjoy surprises.

The four years at Holcim's EMEA Digital Center are the core of it. I was the IdentityIQ technical reference for a platform covering more than 50,000 identities across over 40 countries: joiner-mover-leaver, segregation of duties, the Red Hat SSO estate, the One Identity Safeguard privileged access integration, and the CI/CD pipelines holding all of it together.

These days I am a Senior Consultant at iC Consult and the SailPoint technical reference for the Spanish profit centre. Same job, wider blast radius.

The part I enjoy most is not the platform, it is the handover. I am a certified soft-skills trainer, and I have spent most of my career being the person who gets juniors from "what is an aggregation?" to solving complex tickets on their own. Six months is my current record.
